# Many Turns — Home (fresh Stitch pass)

Desktop landing page for the Many Turns parent house. One composition, journey-first. Institutional trust for partners and high-value clients — not a consulting sales site, not a brand portfolio.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Parent house site for Many Turns (Manyturns OÜ). Institutional, journey-first.
- Colours: Midnight #0B1018, Deep #174D83, Steel #8DCFFF, Ivory #F2F2EF. Light-led ivory page; navy type; steel accents on dark.
- Type: Manrope display, Inter body, mono for turn numbers.
- Logo: winding metallic M mark + “Many Turns” wordmark. Tagline: Different paths. One journey.
- Philosophy: Make the complex simple. Not the simple complex.
- Hero photo: Southeast Asian highlands landscape (owner photo) — full-bleed, no overlays/badges.
- No purple, no glow, no SaaS card grids, no brand portfolio strip, no solutions carousel.
- CTAs: rectangular (not pills). Primary “Begin a conversation” / “Continue this turn →”.

**Page structure:**
1. **Header** — M mark + Many Turns wordmark left; single rectangular CTA “Begin a conversation” right. No mega-nav.
2. **Hero** — Eyebrow tagline. Large brand name “Many Turns” (hero-level). One short support line: paths already walked. Full-bleed highland photograph below or as the visual plane. Brand must still read if nav were removed.
3. **The solution** — Section label + one strong claim: commercial operator and systems thinker — find the real constraint, turn ambiguity into a structure that holds, stay through delivery. Philosophy line as quiet support. 3/9 editorial split OK. No pillar grid.
4. **Journey spine** — Vertical numbered turns 01–08 (mono numbers, hairline path). Short place + title + 1–2 sentence body each.
   - Path-only: 01 The bank, 02 The programme, 03 The network, 06 Wild turns (quieter).
   - Cue turns with accent bar + “Continue this turn →”: 04 The practice → Kavsulting, 05 Built along the way → catalogue story, 07 Life turns → Asia Insights, 08 Room turns → Trivia Deck.
   - Cue labels only (not brand cards). Destination colour accents allowed (steel / Kavsulting steel-grey / Asia teal / Trivia gold) without turning home into a carnival.
5. **Close** — Ivory field. Philosophy as large display. Sam Kavanagh — founder and operator, Europe and Southeast Asia. CTA Begin a conversation.
6. **Footer** — Manyturns OÜ · Estonia; quiet links Advisory, Built, Asia Insights, Trivia Deck.

**Avoid:** dual personal/professional columns, worksheets, stats strips, floating chips on photo, cloning Kavsulting Advise·Coach·Build funnel.
