# Many Turns — Design System (Stitch)

Fresh start for parent hub exploration. Locked product shape from `PROJECT_STATE.md`.

## 1. Role

Institutional operator house for Manyturns OÜ. Journey + solution first. Not a brand portfolio strip. Not a clone of Kavsulting or Asia Insights.

## 2. Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| Midnight | `#0B1018` | Dark fields, hero ground |
| Deep blue | `#174D83` | Primary accent, CTAs on light |
| Steel | `#8DCFFF` | Eyebrows, links on dark |
| Ivory | `#F2F2EF` | Light fields, primary text on dark |

Prefer **light-led page** (ivory / soft warm white) with midnight type and deep/steel accents. Dark hero band OK if photography needs it. No purple gradients, glow, or generic SaaS chrome.

## 3. Typography

- Display / UI: **Manrope** (600–700)
- Body: **Inter** (400–500)
- Meta / turn numbers: **JetBrains Mono** or similar mono
- Tagline: *Different paths. One journey.*
- Philosophy: *Make the complex simple. Not the simple complex.*

## 4. Logo

Protected winding-path **M** only (`public/brand/m-mark.svg`). Not interlocking loops. Not a new mark. Wordmark “Many Turns” beside or under mark — quiet institutional, not carnival.

## 5. Photography

Home hero: Sam’s Asia Insights highland banner (`public/brand/ai-banner.jpg`). Full-bleed or edge-to-edge band. No stock coast. No floating badges on hero media.

## 6. Product shape (do not invent)

1. Hero: brand + tagline + one short line + photography  
2. The solution: one PIP-aligned operator claim + philosophy support  
3. Journey spine: numbered turns 01–08 (path-only quieter; cue turns with Continue)  
4. Close: philosophy + conversation CTA  

**Continue this turn** → venture doors (not stubs). No three-brand card grid on home. No Kavsulting solutions carousel on `/`. Catalogue depth stays on Kavsulting; `/built` is story only.

## 7. Tone

Institutional trust (partners, banks, investors). Operator, not coach-as-headline. Substance over polish. Steal Kavsulting *craft* (claim sharpness, hierarchy, CTA weight) — never consulting IA or near-black sales funnel as parent skin.
